Hinge Proposition
Scharniersatz

A proposition that functions not as a claim subject to epistemic scrutiny, but as a fixed point around which inquiry pivots. Exempt from doubt not by being proven, but by being constitutive of the language-game in which doubt operates.

Agency Mislocation

The systematic error of attributing genuine decision-making and moral agency to algorithmic systems, thereby displacing accountability from the human agents whose judgments are encoded in those systems.

Wittgenstein PI §7
Language Game
Sprachspiel

A complete system of linguistic and non-linguistic practices, including the activities and forms of life in which language is embedded. Meaning is determined by use within a language game, not by reference to mind-independent facts.

Moral Responsibility
Ahlaki Sorumluluk

The property by virtue of which an agent is an appropriate target of reactive attitudes — praise, blame, resentment — in response to their conduct. Requires robust intentionality and the capacity to respond to reasons.

Wittgenstein PI §19
Form of Life
Lebensform

The biological, social, and cultural substrate within which language games are embedded. Forms of life are not justified or explained — they are shown. They are the given from which philosophical investigation must begin.

Opacity Problem
Kara Kutu Sorunu

The epistemic inaccessibility of the internal computational processes of trained neural networks, which creates structural conditions under which accountability attribution becomes practically impossible even where it is in principle required.
