“I am sitting in the garden with a philosopher;
he keeps on pointing to a tree near us and saying,
‘I know that that is a tree.’

Someone comes up and hears this, and I say to him:

‘This fellow isn’t insane.
We are only doing philosophy.’”

— Ludwig Wittgenstein, On Certainty §467
