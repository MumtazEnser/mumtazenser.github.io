A Relational Theory of Responsibility

My work develops a unified philosophical framework for understanding how responsibility, judgment, and authority are displaced within institutional and technological systems.

Drawing on Wittgenstein, Kant, and Heidegger, I argue that what is called the “responsibility gap” in AI ethics is not an absence of responsibility, but a conceptual mislocation produced by institutional forms of rationality.

This framework develops concepts such as authority illusion, ethical withdrawal, and protean responsibility in order to analyze how socio-technical systems reorganize visibility, legitimacy, and accountability.
