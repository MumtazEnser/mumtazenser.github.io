Philosophy of AI, Responsibility, and Normative Structures

Artificial intelligence
does not make decisions.

Responsibility is displaced, distributed, and misrecognized.

I develop a philosophical framework for responsibility, authority, and institutional decision-making in AI systems — showing that the so-called “responsibility gap” is not an absence, but a mislocation.
