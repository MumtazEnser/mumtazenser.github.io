Core Framework

Key Concepts

Authority Illusion

The misrecognition of distributed human responsibility as if it were located in the system itself.

Ethical Withdrawal

The displacement of judgment into procedural structures, allowing responsibility to become anonymous.

Protean Responsibility

Responsibility does not disappear in complex systems; it shifts across design, deployment, and authorization.
