AI, Responsibility & Normativity

Artificial intelligence does not decide.

This research line develops a relational account of responsibility in socio-technical systems, centered on authority illusion, ethical withdrawal, and protean responsibility.

Authority Illusion

Authority illusion refers to the systematic misrecognition of distributed human responsibility as if it were located in the system itself.

Ethical Withdrawal

The displacement of responsibility onto systems or procedures — allowing actors to continue action while responsibility becomes anonymous.

Protean Responsibility

Responsibility does not vanish in complex systems; it shifts shape across design, deployment, and oversight — making it difficult to locate within traditional models.
