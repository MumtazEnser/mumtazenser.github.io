Abstract

Authority illusion names the misrecognition of institutional and collective authority as though it originated from the artificial system itself. Artificial intelligence systems do not independently possess normative authority; institutional actors project authority onto outputs that are subsequently treated as objective, neutral, or inevitable. The result is a displacement of responsibility from human practices toward technological artifacts.

Conceptual Function

This concept is part of a broader framework concerning AI governance, institutional responsibility, Wittgensteinian critique, and the conditions under which judgment remains visible or withdraws from practice.
