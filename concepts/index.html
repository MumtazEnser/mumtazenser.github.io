Concepts

Ethical Withdrawal

Ethical Withdrawal occurs when actors recognize a morally significant harm but cease to treat it as a reason to question, reconsider, or intervene because procedural compliance is regarded as sufficient.

Authority Illusion

Authority Illusion arises when AI outputs are treated as though they independently settle questions of judgment, concealing the human and institutional practices through which those outputs acquire authority.

Protean Responsibility

Protean Responsibility describes how responsibility becomes attributable and changes form as AI outputs move through practices of acceptance, validation, and authorization.

Artificial Ontology

Artificial Ontology describes the misrecognition of functional necessity as natural rank, through which socially established positions appear to possess a natural rather than constructed standing.
