“No relation of power is free from exposure.”

Abstract

Human relations are simultaneously structured by dependence, antagonism, and unequal distributions of power. No social relation is entirely free from vulnerability, negotiation, or domination. This framework examines how political and institutional orders emerge not from harmony or rational consensus alone, but from unstable relations shaped by mutual exposure and structural asymmetry.

Conceptual Function

This concept is part of a broader framework concerning AI governance, institutional responsibility, Wittgensteinian critique, and the conditions under which judgment remains visible or withdraws from practice.
