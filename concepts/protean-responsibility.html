Abstract

Responsibility does not disappear within AI systems; it changes form, disperses across institutional structures, and becomes difficult to localize. Protean responsibility names the dynamic transformation of responsibility across stages such as data collection, model design, optimization, implementation, deployment, and institutional interpretation. The so-called responsibility gap is often a category mistake generated by the misdescription of distributed institutional agency.

Conceptual Function

This concept is part of a broader framework concerning AI governance, institutional responsibility, Wittgensteinian critique, and the conditions under which judgment remains visible or withdraws from practice.
