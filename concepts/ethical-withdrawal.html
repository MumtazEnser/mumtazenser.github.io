“Reason remains; judgment withdraws.”

Abstract

Ethical withdrawal describes a condition in which visible harm ceases to function as a practical interruption because procedural correctness replaces ethical judgment. Modern institutional systems frequently preserve rational procedure while suspending lived responsibility. The problem is therefore not the disappearance of reason, but the social organization of judgment in ways that permit responsibility to withdraw while maintaining the appearance of legitimacy.

Conceptual Function

This concept is part of a broader framework concerning AI governance, institutional responsibility, Wittgensteinian critique, and the conditions under which judgment remains visible or withdraws from practice.
